Selected work

ORBISQuantum hardwareOrbital Remediation Quantum Intelligence System: choosing and sequencing debris to remove.

Screens 15,982 tracked objects from Space-Track using SGP4 propagation, then runs a 72-hour conjunction analysis with a KD-tree and the Chan 2008 collision probability method. Targets are picked by a diversity-aware score across orbital regimes.

A physics-informed network, DeltaVNet, approximates manoeuvre delta-v. Sequencing is written as a QUBO and solved with QAOA, against a simulated annealing baseline.

n=4, 16 qubits100%
n=5, 25 qubits110.5 ± 9.7%
n=6, 36 qubits115.9 ± 18.7%
n=8, 64 qubits195.1 ± 12.2%

Recorded QPU results by problem size, with the n=4 run as the 100% reference. Hardware noise grows with circuit depth, and quality drops sharply at the larger sizes.

SAR to opticalDeep learningTranslating Sentinel-1 radar patches into optical-style imagery with Pix2Pix.

16,000 paired patches over four terrains, split by scene (21 train, 4 validation, 5 test) so no scene appears in two sets. I compared the baseline against an L1-only ablation and a variant with spatial attention and VGG19 perceptual loss. Neither variant beat the baseline, and I report that as it is.

Cuprite mineralsHyperspectralMineral mapping from AVIRIS shortwave infrared data over Cuprite, Nevada.

Works on the 1900 to 2470 nm range against the USGS spectral library. The pipeline extracts endmembers with a VCA-style method, applies continuum removal, matches with Spectral Angle Mapper against several references, and masks low-confidence pixels. Buddingtonite is folded into the montmorillonite family because its reference spectra mix.

Dynamic news RAGGenAIQuestion answering grounded in freshly retrieved news, not a fixed corpus.

A query pulls current articles from NewsAPI, which are chunked, embedded with SentenceTransformers and indexed in FAISS. The top matches go to an LLM through Groq or OpenAI so answers stay tied to the retrieved text. BM25 and retrieval evaluation components support comparison, and a Gradio interface sits on top.
